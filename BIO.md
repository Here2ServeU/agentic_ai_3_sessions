# About Your Instructor — Emmanuel Naweji

Hi, I'm **Emmanuel Naweji**. I'm a DevOps, Cloud, Infrastructure, SRE
 and AI engineer, and I built this course to help complete beginners feel at home with
something that can sound scary at first: **AI agents in DevOps.**

## My Journey

I didn't start out knowing any of this. Like a lot of people, I began with
small questions and a lot of curiosity. One step at a time, I learned how to
run servers, build pipelines, manage the cloud, and keep big systems healthy.
The more I learned, the more I noticed something: the hard part is rarely the
technology itself. The hard part is finding someone who will explain it in
plain words, without making you feel left behind.

Over the years I've worked across all three major clouds — **AWS, Azure, and
Google Cloud** — and with the tools that hold modern systems together, like
**Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Prometheus, and
Grafana.** Today a lot of my focus is on the newest piece of the puzzle:
teaching AI to help us do this work faster and more safely.

I'm also pursuing a **PhD in Computer Science**, with a focus on **AI and
Machine Learning, and Robotics in Healthcare, Cloud, and DevOps.** That research keeps me
close to the cutting edge, and reminds me every day how powerful these ideas
become when they're explained simply.

## Why I Teach

The best part of my work isn't the code, it's the moment a learner says, "Oh!
Now I get it." I love mentoring beginners and walking with people from "I have
no idea what this means" to "I just built my first AI agent." That's exactly
what this course is for.

I believe **anyone** can learn this. You don't need a fancy degree or years of
experience. You need clear steps, a little patience, and someone in your corner.
That's the promise of this course, and it's the promise I make to every person
I mentor.
